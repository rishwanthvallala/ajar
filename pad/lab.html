Completion lab

Small code-completion models, running in this browser and nowhere else. Load one, and it is downloaded once and kept by the browser. Then the test cases ask each loaded model what goes at a cursor in the middle of a line, and the playground asks as you type.

Models

Test cases

Each case is a cursor in the middle of a line, and the line a person would finish it with. A suggestion counts as right when its first line is that line exactly. Kept is how much of the line it gets right from the start, which is what pressing Tab would keep. CodeT5 writes load ( f) for load(f); tidied, those spaces go, and what it wrote is shown when you point at a suggestion.

Playground

Type, and stop: each loaded model suggests what comes at the cursor. Tab takes the one picked.
